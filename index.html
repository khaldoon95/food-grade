THE FOUR TIERS
HOW FOOD GRADE CLASSIFIES FOOD
INCREDIBLE
Build your diet around these. High satiety, strong nutrition, efficient calories. Eat freely.
STRATEGIC
Good food, worth eating — with some awareness. Great nutrition but watch the quantity or timing.
MODERATION
Not bad, not great. Fine occasionally. Won't move the needle either way.
AVOID
Low nutrition, high cost, easy to overeat. Not worth your calories.
HOW THE SCORE WORKS
THREE AXES, ONE NUMBER
SAT
How filling is this food per calorie?
35% of score
PRO
Protein content and quality
35% of score
MIC
Vitamins, minerals, micronutrient density
30% of score
The score is optimised for eating well without overthinking it — prioritising foods that fill you up, build muscle, and cover your micronutrients efficiently. It is not a universal health score. Calorie-dense foods like olive oil score lower here, not because they're unhealthy, but because they serve a different purpose.

YOUR MODE
SAME FOOD, DIFFERENT GOAL, DIFFERENT ANSWER
✂ CUT
Satiety matters most. Foods that fill you up on fewer calories rank higher. Score weights: SAT 40% / PRO 35% / MIC 25%.
◎ MAINTAIN
Balanced weights. Default mode. Score: SAT 35% / PRO 35% / MIC 30%.
▲ BULK
Satiety works against you here. Calorie-dense nutritious foods rank higher. Score: SAT 20% / PRO 40% / MIC 40%.
Switching mode recalculates every score and shifts some tiers. Salmon goes Incredible on Bulk. Boiled Potato drops to Strategic. Rice goes up. The same food, genuinely different value depending on your goal.
